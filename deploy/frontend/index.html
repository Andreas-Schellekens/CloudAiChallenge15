    Never decide whether to eat a mushroom with this tool.

    It is a student model trained on a deliberately noisy dataset, where about 2% of the labels are flipped. It is tuned to catch 90% of poisonous mushrooms, and pays for that by flagging roughly two thirds of the edible ones as poisonous too.
