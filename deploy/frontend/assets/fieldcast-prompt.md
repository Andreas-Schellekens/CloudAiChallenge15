## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Create a finished brand identity presentation for "fieldcast", an interactive prediction studio exploring mushrooms and city bicycle demand. This is one coherent logo, not a grid of options.
Design direction: confident contemporary independent software brand, typographic and architectural, warm intelligence, visually memorable. A bold custom lowercase wordmark "fieldcast" in rounded geometric sans typography, carefully kerned, medium-heavy with subtly customized f and a. To its left an original compact abstract F symbol built from two or three substantial curved parallel ribbons, suggesting branching paths, field contour lines and forward prediction. The symbol must feel designed as a letterform rather than a familiar stock icon. Strong simplified silhouette, visibly related to the wordmark. No leaf, no neural network nodes, no circuits, no mushroom or bicycle pictograms.
Composition: wide horizontal main logo lockup centered in the upper two thirds of a generous warm off-white canvas #f3f2ed. Main symbol vivid periwinkle-cobalt #6269f5, wordmark very dark charcoal #17191f. Beneath it one small standalone version of exactly the same symbol, centered, to demonstrate the app icon at small scale. Add tiny restrained subtitle "OBSERVE. MODEL. EXPLORE." below the main wordmark in spaced uppercase, charcoal. Main logo occupies most of the width with generous margins. All text must be exact.
Style: exceptionally clean crisp flat graphic design, precise edges, deliberate proportions, no gradients, no 3D, no mockup objects, no textures, no lighting effects, no shadows, no watermarks. This should look like a professional identity designer's first chosen direction, not a technology clip-art logo.
